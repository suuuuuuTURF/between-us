How it works.

No complicated setup. No endless features. Just two people, one room, and the music they want to share.

01

START HERE

Create a room.

Enter your name and give your little room a name. Between Us will create a unique room for the two of you.

02

INVITE THEM

Share the room.

Once your room is created, you'll receive a unique code and a shareable link. Send either one to the person you want to listen with.

03

TWO PEOPLE

They join you.

They enter their name and your room code. Once they're inside, you'll both be connected to the same listening room.

04

CHOOSE A SONG

Bring the music.

Copy the YouTube link of the song you want to hear. Paste it into the room and press play.

05

TOGETHER

Listen at the same moment.

When one person plays, pauses, or moves through the song, the other screen follows along. Same song. Same moment.

06

SAY IT WITHOUT WORDS

Send a little reaction.

Tap a heart, a smile, or whatever you're feeling. Your reaction appears on the other person's screen. No chat needed.

That's it.

Two places. One song.
